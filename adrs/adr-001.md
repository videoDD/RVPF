# ADR-001: Separation of Business State and Hardware Communication

## Status
Accepted

## Context
In standard Virtual Production setups, real-time engines often process technical inputs (e.g., MIDI, DMX, OSC, LiveLink) directly within the device logic or Blueprint actors. This tight coupling makes devices rigid, difficult to swap, and conflates semantic business logic with technical protocol parsing. We need a way to represent an entity within the framework without binding it to its physical hardware protocol.

## Decision
The `StudioObject` will act exclusively as an Aggregate Root that models the semantic business state of a production entity. It will only contain generic properties such as Identity, Role, Lifecycle, and Capabilities. All communication with actual hardware, protocols, or external systems will be handled strictly by separate, modular `Components` attached to the `StudioObject`.

## Consequences
* **Positive:** Complete hardware-agnosticism. A virtual Unreal Engine light and a physical DMX ARRI SkyPanel share the exact same `StudioObject` representation.
* **Positive:** High reusability and isolated testability of the semantic layer.
* **Negative:** Slight increase in architectural overhead, as developers cannot write direct hardware calls inside the main object, but must route them through the component architecture.
