## 1. Context
Virtual Production environments continuously receive data from heterogeneous sources (faders, spatial trackers, network sockets, physical buttons). Treating these raw data points as immediate business actions creates brittle, protocol-dependent logic. For example, a spatial coordinate from a tracking cluster `(2.31, 1.74, 0.85)` is merely a numeric measurement vector with no innate production meaning.

---

## 2. Decision
The RVPF initially processes all incoming external and internal information strictly as normalized, semantically neutral **Impulses** (`FStudioImpulse`). An impulse carries no business logic or semantic meaning; it represents an immutable, standardized temporal data point. Semantic meaning is established exclusively downstream when the impulse is evaluated against the active production context.

---

## 3. Consequences
* **Positive:** Complete decoupling of physical data ingestion from production business logic.
* **Positive:** A uniform, protocol-agnostic entry point across all inputs (MIDI note, DMX value, OSC bundle, tracking coordinate).
* **Positive:** Establishes a deterministic boundary between technical measurements and contextual meaning.
* **Negative:** Requires an explicit normalization and validation ingestion step prior to state evaluation.
